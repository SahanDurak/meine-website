A sketch is a quick way to think on paper. Good drawings and good machines share one habit: they start rough and get refined, as described in the [MDX documentation](https://mdxjs.com) for content like this, and a single `inline code` snippet should sit calmly inside the line.

## Sketching and engineering

Both fields reward patience. A sketch shows an idea before it is finished, and a prototype shows whether the idea holds up.

### What a sketch is good for

- Testing a shape before committing to it
- Showing a problem to someone else
- Remembering a thought that would otherwise be lost

### How a small project can proceed

1. Draw the rough idea.
2. Build the smallest working version.
3. Measure what happens.
4. Refine and repeat.

> A drawing is not the thing itself, but it is often the fastest way to find out what the thing should be.

Some words in a sentence carry **strong** weight, while others only need _emphasis_. The difference should be visible without being loud.
